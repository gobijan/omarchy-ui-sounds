quake — LibreQuake samples
==========================

Not id Software Quake data. These are original remakes from LibreQuake,
a free replacement for Quake's game files.

Source: https://github.com/lavenderdotpet/LibreQuake
License: BSD 3-Clause (docs/COPYING in LibreQuake)
Art/sounds are BSD-3-Clause; do not confuse with QuakeC (GPL).
